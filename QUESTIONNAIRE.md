# ShipSpark

### HackOnVibe Questionnaire

**Know when a release deserves attention.**

</div>

---

### ⚡ 1. What does your application/service do?

ShipSpark helps mobile teams decide whether a release is actually worth promoting.

It analyzes available evidence from:

* App Store
* Google Play
* Recent customer reviews
* Release notes
* Optional public GitHub activity

It then returns one clear decision:

**PROMOTE**  
The release has meaningful user value and enough customer relevance to justify promotion.

**WAIT**  
There may be an opportunity, but the timing or evidence is not strong enough yet.

**SKIP**  
The release does not currently deserve marketing attention.

Each analysis includes an opportunity score, confidence, six evidence scores, score explanations, customer insights, release insights, risks, and a recommended next action.

When the result is **PROMOTE**, ShipSpark also generates campaign content, supports native social sharing with optional link and image media, and can publish the prepared Discord message through a real Discord webhook.

**Evidence → Decision → Explanation → Campaign → Publish**

---

### 👥 2. Who is the target audience?

ShipSpark is built for mobile teams that release often but do not want to market every update.

The main users are:

* Indie developers
* Mobile app founders
* Product managers
* Growth teams
* Mobile marketers
* Developer led startups
* Small mobile product teams

The strongest early customer is a small or medium team where engineering, product, and growth responsibilities overlap.

### 🆚 4. Who are your competitors?

ShipSpark overlaps with several existing categories rather than having one exact direct competitor.

**AppFollow**  
App review intelligence, reputation management, and customer feedback.

**Appbot**  
Review monitoring and sentiment analysis.

**AppTweak**  
App Store Optimization and mobile market intelligence.

**App Radar**  
App Store Optimization and app growth tooling.

ShipSpark also competes indirectly with generic AI marketing tools and manual release review workflows.

The difference is the question ShipSpark focuses on:

> **Should this specific release be promoted at all?**

---

### 🚀 5. What is your advantage?

ShipSpark puts the **decision before the marketing**.

Most AI marketing workflows begin with:

> What should we write?

ShipSpark begins with:

> **Is this release actually worth talking about?**

It combines available customer reviews, release notes, store positioning, customer demand, optional GitHub activity, user facing changes, timing, and evidence quality.

Then it compares:

**What customers care about**

with:

**What actually shipped**

and returns:

**PROMOTE, WAIT, or SKIP**

Every score is explained.

Campaign generation only happens when promotion is justified.

If the result is PROMOTE, ShipSpark can continue directly into campaign creation, native social sharing, and real Discord publishing.

The main advantage is:

> **ShipSpark decides whether a campaign should exist before writing one.**
